What is CrossFit?

CrossFit is a principal strength and conditioning program designed for constant variation, high-intensity functional movements. At CrossFit Cainta East Ortigas, under Head Coach Jason Truman, we help athletes build strength, stamina and confidence in a welcoming, disciplined environment.
