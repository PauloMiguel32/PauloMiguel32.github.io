What is CrossFit?

CrossFit is a principal strength and conditioning program designed for constant variation, high-intensity functional movements. At CrossFit Cainta East Ortigas, under Head Coach Jason Truman, we forge real functional fitness across cardiovascular endurance, stamina, strength, flexibility, power, speed, coordination, and agility.
